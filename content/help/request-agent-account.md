Getting started with Deedo is self-serve and free — you create your own agent account, and your first listing is free for 30 days. Because Deedo is a professional tool for licensed real estate agents, we verify your identity and license before the account is fully active. This guide walks you through the whole process.

## Before you start

Have these ready so you can finish in one sitting:

- **A work email address** you can access (for confirmation and account notices).
- **Your real estate license details** — you'll be asked to verify that you're a licensed agent, or to confirm your identity.
- **About 5 minutes.**

## How to create your agent account

1. **Go to the signup page.** Open [app.deedo.ai/signup](https://app.deedo.ai/signup) in your browser.
2. **Create your account.** Enter your name, your work email address, and a password. Using your professional email helps verification go smoothly.
3. **Confirm your email.** We'll send a confirmation link to the address you entered — open the email and click the link to verify it. (If it doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam or promotions folder.)
4. **Verify your identity and license.** Follow the prompts to confirm your real estate license or verify your identity. This step keeps the platform trusted for agents, buyers, and their brokers.
5. **Set up your first listing.** Once your account is verified, you can add your first property and put Deedo's AI concierge on it — free for your first 30 days.

## What happens after you sign up

After you submit your details, your account goes through a quick verification review. You'll receive an email as soon as your agent account is active. Until then, you can sign in, but some features stay locked until verification is complete.

## Troubleshooting

- **The confirmation email didn't arrive.** Check your spam and promotions folders, confirm you entered the right address, and allow a few minutes for delivery. You can request a new confirmation email from the signup screen.
- **My verification is still pending.** Verification is usually quick. If it's taking longer than expected, make sure any requested license or identity details were submitted, then reach out to us (see the FAQ below).
- **I already have an account.** Head to [app.deedo.ai](https://app.deedo.ai) and sign in instead of creating a new one.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is a Deedo agent account free?

Yes — creating an account is free, and your first listing is free for 30 days with no credit card required.

### What do I need to verify my account?

You'll be asked to confirm that you're a licensed real estate agent or to verify your identity. Having your license details on hand makes this fast.

### How long does verification take?

Most accounts are verified quickly, and you'll get an email the moment yours is active. If it's taking longer than expected, contact us and we'll help.

### Can I sign up with a personal email?

You can, but a professional work email is recommended — it makes verification smoother and keeps your account communications in one place.

### Who do I contact if I need help?

If you get stuck at any step, reach out through our [contact page](/talk/) and the Deedo team will help you get set up.

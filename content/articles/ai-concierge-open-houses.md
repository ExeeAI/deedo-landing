Open houses generate a flood of the same questions — HOA fees, year built, roof age, school district — and most of the people asking never leave a phone number. An **AI concierge** flips that dynamic: it answers every buyer question instantly, around the clock, using only the documents you've uploaded, and captures a verified lead for every serious conversation.

Here's what that looks like in practice, and why it changes the economics of an open house.

## Answers that come from your documents, not the open web

The core of a trustworthy AI concierge is that it's **grounded**. Instead of guessing or pulling from the open internet, it answers strictly from the PDFs, disclosures, and specs you upload for that specific listing. Ask it "What are the HOA fees?" and it replies "$320/month, per the HOA disclosure" — with the source and a confidence score.

That grounding matters for two reasons:

- **Accuracy** — buyers get correct information, and you're not liable for an AI inventing details.
- **Trust** — every answer shows its confidence, and when a question is subjective or unanswerable, the AI defers to you instead of making something up.

## It works while you don't

A buyer who scans your yard sign at 9pm on a Sunday doesn't wait until Monday. The concierge is live 24/7, so the listing keeps selling itself long after the open house ends. You stop repeating yourself, and you stop losing the late-night, high-intent browsers who would otherwise move on.

## Every serious question becomes a verified lead

This is the part that pays for itself. When a buyer engages, they authenticate with a phone-first, one-time SMS code — so you capture a **real, verified US phone number**, not a fake email. Interest gets tagged automatically (Very Interested, Considering, Browsing), and when a question needs a human, the AI hands off to you with a two-line summary.

The result: instead of a clipboard of half-legible names, you get a dashboard of qualified buyers ranked by intent.

## Getting started takes minutes

Setting a listing up is fast:

1. Upload the documents you already have.
2. Pass an automated readiness check that scores whether the AI has enough to be accurate.
3. Deploy a QR code on your sign, flyers, and listing — buyers scan to start.

## The takeaway

An AI concierge doesn't replace you — it removes the repetitive Q&A that eats your day and lets you focus on the buyers who are ready to move. Grounded answers build trust, 24/7 availability captures demand you were losing, and verified lead capture turns curiosity into a pipeline.
